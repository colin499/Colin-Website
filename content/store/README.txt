Things for sale go in here: a photo plus a .md note with the same name.
In the note's top block, "price:" is the price and "link:" is the page
where people pay. Leave "link:" out and the button emails you instead.
A folder called "not-shown" in here is ignored, so you can keep things
there that aren't for sale right now.
See "Add something to the store" in HOW-TO-EDIT.md.
